ABOUT US

Our Mission is Their Future

LumiNest Foundation is a non-profit organization dedicated to providing a safe, loving and nurturing home for motherless and vulnerable children. We believe every child deserves care, dignity, and the chance to reach their full potential.

Our Goal

To give every child a safe place to grow.

Our Vision

A world where no child feels alone or unloved.

Our Values

Compassion, Integrity, Accountability, Hope.

OUR PROGRAMS

How We Help

We provide comprehensive care and support to ensure every child is healthy, happy and prepared for the future.

Infant Care

(0–2 years)

Specialized 24/7 care, pediatric nutrition, and early bonding support for motherless newborns and babies.

Child Care

(3–12 years)

A nurturing, family-like environment with recreational activities, social integration, and guidance.

Education & Development

All Ages

Quality schooling, tutoring, creative workshops, and skill-building programs for future independence.

Healthcare & Wellness

Continuous

Regular medical checkups, mental health counseling, vaccination drives, and clean living conditions.
