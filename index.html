ABOUT US

Our Mission is Their Future

LumiNest Foundation is a non-profit organization dedicated to providing a safe, loving and nurturing home for motherless and vulnerable children. We believe every child deserves care, dignity, protection and the chance to reach their full potential.

♧

Our Goal

Give every child a safe place to grow.

♡

Our Vision

A world where no child feels alone or unloved.
